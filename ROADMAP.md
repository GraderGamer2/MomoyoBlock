# Roadmap

## 0.1 · Surface to orbit

Walk and mine on Cinder Reach, travel through a beacon to Relay Nine, build on the station, and return to the surface. Save the expedition and both locations' edits. Package and test the first Linux x86_64 build.

## Next · Building across worlds

- Add a second landable world with a reason to carry materials between locations.
- Prototype craftable equipment and station pieces that work with the existing block grid.
- Test longer construction sessions, faster movement across chunk boundaries, and frame pacing on the target Linux machine.

## Later · Space travel and station life

- Decide how hands-on launch, docking, and travel should be. The prototype uses a short transfer.
- Explore walkable ship interiors, extendable stations, and room systems as building earns more depth.
- Choose the texture and animation direction after testing the scale and readability of the blocks.
